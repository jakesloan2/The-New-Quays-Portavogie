Common questions

Before you visit

How do I book a table at The New Quays?

Tables are booked by telephone on 028 4277 2225. There is no online booking system at present. If you are eight or more, celebrating something, bringing a dog or have a dietary requirement, mention it when you ring and we will be ready for you.

Where is The New Quays in Portavogie?

81 New Harbour Road, Portavogie, County Down, BT22 1EB — about a hundred yards from Portavogie harbour, on the east coast of the Ards Peninsula.

What food does The New Quays serve?

Seafood is the focus, including the Portavogie prawn landed by the local fleet. Alongside it there are grills, a children’s menu, vegetarian options and a Sunday roast, which received a Knorr Recommended Roast 2026 award. See the full menu.

Is there parking?

Yes — free parking on site, with further parking at the harbour car park a short walk away.

Is The New Quays dog friendly?

Yes. Dogs are welcome and there is outdoor seating on the terrace. Let us know when you ring.

What are the opening hours?

Besties Café opens Thursday to Saturday, 10am to 3pm, and Sunday 10am to 12pm. Restaurant hours: [OPENING HOURS].

What is Besties Café?

Besties is our café, in its own room inside the same building. It serves breakfast, brunch, sandwiches, scones and traybakes, and shares our phone number. More about Besties.

Seafood, a working harbour and a warm welcome on the east coast of the Ards Peninsula.
