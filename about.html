Our story

About The New Quays

A seafood restaurant in a working fishing village, on the easternmost edge of Ireland.

Portavogie has been a fishing settlement for centuries. The boats that work out of the harbour catch mainly prawns and herring, and most evenings the fish is auctioned on the quay.

The New Quays sits a hundred yards from all of it, on New Harbour Road, with the Irish Sea on one side of the building and the harbour on the other. That proximity is not a marketing line. It is the reason the restaurant is here and the reason the menu looks the way it does.

The kitchen

Cooked to order, changed by the tide

Whitefish, shellfish and the celebrated Portavogie prawn come off the local boats. What is available depends on the season and on what has been landed, so specials change.

Alongside the seafood there are grills, a children’s menu, vegetarian options and a Sunday roast that was recognised with a Knorr Recommended Roast 2026 award.
